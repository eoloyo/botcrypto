Finding

What stops Simpl-Open from federating with any data space is a decision, not missing technology.

  1. The target is law. has applied since 12 September 2025, and (April 2026) is the first standard of the harmonised set behind it. Conforming will count as compliance.
  2. The building blocks exist. , , DCAT-AP and ODRL as pivots; , and piveau as running EU code; as a federation pattern in production.
  3. One piece is missing everywhere: explicit, testable handling of what gets lost in translation: a , a , and governed proxy vs identity-preserving mediation. That is where Simpl can contribute.
  4. The technical distance is small, and now demonstrated. In the lab, Simpl's connector moved to EDC 0.11 with three pom lines, switched its identity to DCP, and , with Simpl's own ABAC rule deciding on the credential's claims. Untrusted issuers, missing credentials and insufficient attributes are rejected.

What follows: revisit the pairwise trust model of ADR 008, extend DCP to machine identity, set Art. 33 / EN 18235 conformance as the target in EIF terms, build from EU building blocks and EDC, and contribute the fidelity contract. Confidence: the legal, standards, OOTS and lab results are checked or run; a few ecosystem items (Catena-X, iSHARE) rest on background knowledge; the lab uses did:web over http and one shared IdentityHub, not a production deployment.
